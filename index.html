Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Which country is best for MBBS abroad for Kerala students?▼
Egypt and Uzbekistan are most popular for Kerala students due to affordability (₹3–4L/year), NMC recognition, English-medium teaching and large Indian student communities. Georgia offers European quality with visa-free entry for Indians. Kazakhstan is a strong mid-range option. Bulgaria provides an EU-recognised degree. Contact EduCalling for personalised guidance.
How much does MBBS abroad cost in total?▼
Total MBBS cost (tuition + hostel + food + visa) varies: Uzbekistan ₹26–29L, Egypt ₹38–61L, Kazakhstan ₹32–44L, Georgia ₹39–57L, Bulgaria ₹78–84L (EU degree). Claims of ₹10L MBBS programs are likely scams.
Is NEET required for MBBS abroad?▼
Most foreign medical universities don't require a NEET score for admission. However, to practise medicine in India after your MBBS abroad, you must qualify the FMGE/NMC screening exam. NEET qualifying score in 2025 was 144 (General) and 113 (OBC/SC/ST). NEET scores are valid for 3 years.
Are all universities NMC and WHO recognised?▼
Yes — EduCalling Global LLP exclusively recommends NMC and WHO recognised universities. All 22 universities listed on this website (across Egypt, Uzbekistan, Kazakhstan, Georgia and Bulgaria) are fully approved by NMC and WHO, ensuring your degree is valid for FMGE and international practice.
What is the FMGE pass rate for these universities?▼
Bukhara State Medical Institute had the best FMGE pass rate in Uzbekistan in 2024 — 47.83%. Samarkand State Medical University also performs well. Georgia universities like Tbilisi State Medical University have growing FMGE pass rates. EduCalling provides dedicated FMGE preparation guidance to all students.
What is the advantage of a Bulgaria EU degree?▼
A Bulgaria EU-recognised medical degree allows you to practise medicine in all 27 European Union countries after completing EU licensing requirements (e.g., PLAB in UK, USMLE in USA, or working directly in EU). It is also NMC recognised for returning to India. Bulgaria offers European-standard education at a much lower cost than UK, USA or Germany.
